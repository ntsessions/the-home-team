Our story

Made for the moms in the bleachers

We watched how hard it was for sports moms to get real time with their kids, and we wanted to make a difference.

Between early practices, late games, and weekend tournaments, the moms we talked to were with their athletes all the time. They were driving them, feeding them, and cheering from the stands. But when we asked about the time they actually got with their kids, we kept hearing the same thing: there wasn't much of it, and it didn't feel very meaningful.

The car ride turned into phones and headphones. Dinner turned into "how was practice?" and "fine." The season flew by.

So we made something simple. The Home Team is a deck of conversation starters and 30-minute activities that fits in the glovebox, sits on the dinner table, and comes along to the game. No prep, no planning, no screens. Just pull a card and spend a little time together that actually counts.
